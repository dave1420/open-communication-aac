# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, accessible, and harassment-free community. Treat AAC users as experts in their own communication. Listen without demanding disclosure of diagnoses or private medical details.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, infantilization, publishing private information, sexualized attention, intimidation, and dismissing accessibility needs without evidence.

Project maintainers may remove contributions or participation that violate these expectations. A private conduct-reporting address and a fuller enforcement policy will be added before accepting public contributions.
