# Privacy

## Prototype defaults

- No account is required.
- No message content is saved after the page closes.
- No analytics, advertising, tracking pixels, or remote AI services are included.
- Access preferences are stored locally in the browser.
- Custom phrase buttons are stored locally in the browser so they remain available after the app closes.
- Speech uses the device/browser speech-synthesis facility. Available voices and platform behavior vary.
- Voices labelled **installed** stay on the device. Selecting a voice labelled **online** may send the text being spoken to the browser provider's speech service.
- Word suggestions are generated locally from bundled vocabulary and words used during the current session. Session words are not retained after the page closes.
- The expanded English and Canadian spelling dictionary is bundled with the app and does not make network requests.

## Contributions and testing

Do not submit real patient information, private conversations, clinical records, personal photographs, voice recordings, addresses, telephone numbers, or other identifying data in source code, issues, screenshots, tests, or sample boards.

Future sync, backup, telemetry, or research features require a separate privacy and threat-model review before implementation.
