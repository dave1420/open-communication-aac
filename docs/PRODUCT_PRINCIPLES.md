# Product Principles

## The communicator leads

The person using the application chooses their language, vocabulary, symbols, voice, layout, and privacy boundaries. Family members and professionals support that choice rather than replacing it.

## Adult competence is visible

The interface must not infantilize adults. Medical and basic-needs phrases are necessary, but social closeness, humour, opinions, disagreement, consent, refusal, and ordinary conversation are equally important.

## Access can change

Direct touch is one access method, not an architectural assumption. The application should support touch, pointer, keyboard, switch scanning, dwell, and compatible eye-gaze workflows through a consistent focus model.

## Communication is private

Messages are not telemetry. The default product has no account, advertising, analytics, cloud language model, or retained message history. Any future sync or research feature must be optional, understandable, revocable, and separately reviewed.

## Failure must be graceful

The core board works without internet access. A user can undo errors, export their board, recover it on another device, and fall back to a matching printed board.

## Clinical collaboration without gatekeeping

SLP and occupational-therapy input improves feature matching, positioning, and access choices. The free application remains available without requiring a diagnosis or professional subscription.
